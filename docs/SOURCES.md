# Where every fact on this site came from

The site makes a promise in its own footer: it does not guess at a price,
invent today's specials, take money online, or show reviews nobody wrote. This
file is how that promise is kept honest. If a claim on the page is not
traceable to a row below, it should not be on the page.

## What is deliberately absent

- **No `aggregateRating` and no reviews.** The shop is new and has none. A
  rating computed from nothing would go straight into Google's results.
- **No geo coordinates in the structured data.** Nobody has stood at the door
  with a GPS. The map is centred on the street, which is honest about its own
  precision.
- **No distance in metres** to the school, the library or the station. "A short
  walk" is as precise as anyone has actually measured.
- **No certificate or verification number** from the licence, on the page or in
  the assistant's knowledge. It is a document identifier, not customer
  information.
- **No claim about the food's quality.** Nobody who built this has eaten there.
